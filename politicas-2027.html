Participants must be in good physical condition, without recent surgery, heart conditions, pregnancy, disability or injury. Inform your guide about required medication or other medical concerns.

No nos hacemos responsables de nada de lo anterior.Costa Rica’s natural environment includes insects, snakes, crocodiles and other wildlife, as well as possible landslides, rockfalls and flash floods.
